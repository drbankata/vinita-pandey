Privacy notice

How your personal information is looked after.

Vinita Pandey (“I”, “me”) is a self-employed homeopath practising at The Health Zone Clinic, 30 Wimbledon Hill Road, London SW19 7PA, and online. I am the data controller for the personal information described here.

What I collect

  • When you contact me or request an appointment: your name, email, phone number, preferred times and anything you choose to write.
  • When you become a client: the health and personal information you share in consultations, recorded in confidential case notes.
  • Payments: handled by the payment provider you choose; I do not see or store your card details.

Why, and on what basis

I use your information to reply to you, arrange and provide consultations, and keep the records a professional homeopath must keep. Health information is processed with your explicit consent and to provide health care under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

How messages are sent from this website

This website has no database. When you send a form, the message is passed to your own email app or messaging app (or a secure form service) for you to send. The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

I never sell your information. I share it only when you ask me to (for example with your GP), when required by law, or with services I use to run my practice (email, secure storage, payments) under appropriate safeguards.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment are deleted within twelve months. Clinical records are kept for eight years after your last consultation (or until a child turns 25), in line with professional guidance, then securely destroyed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, at any time — just get in touch. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
