Practical details

Before you book

How do I pay?

Once your appointment is confirmed, you can pay by card online, by bank transfer, or by card at the clinic. You’ll receive a receipt for every payment.

How do online consultations work?

You’ll receive a private video link by email before your appointment — no special software needed. Find a quiet, comfortable spot where you won’t be interrupted. Remedies are posted to you, or you can collect them from a homeopathic pharmacy.

What if I need to change my appointment?

Of course — just let Vinita know at least 24 hours before. Appointments missed or cancelled with less notice may be charged in full.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Nothing formal. Some people like to jot down their main concerns, any medicines and supplements they take, and questions they want to ask.

Is my information confidential?

Completely. Everything you share stays between you and Vinita, and your notes are stored securely, in line with UK data protection law. See the privacy notice.
