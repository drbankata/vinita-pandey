Holistic wellness

Detoxification & healing with homeopathy

Releasing what clings to us on every level — physical, mental, emotional and spiritual — and how homeopathy approaches detox.

Dr Vinita Pandey PhD, LCHE, RSHom
3 January 2021 · 5 min read

Detoxification is the process of removing toxic waste substances from the body. When we talk about doing a detox we are normally referring to an age-old naturopathic process of cleaning the blood. Various detox programmes support the body’s natural cleaning process: resting the organs through fasting, supporting the liver, and promoting elimination through the intestines, kidneys and skin. The idea is to improve circulation, release stagnation, refuel the body with healthy nutrients and recharge.

Detoxifying, to me, means expelling everything that is clinging onto us at all levels: physical, mental, emotional and spiritual. We all need to absorb what is good and strong, and discard what makes us weak. One positive charge leads to an influx of many more; one negative charge leads to a stream of negatives. So it is important to focus on goodness and strength while letting go of stress, fear and weakness.

As technology improves and life becomes more modern, many of us feel our health slipping — and that is something each of us can address. We are reservoirs of infinite energy, each capable of lighting up a whole city, provided we put that energy in the right direction.

Stress affects our health in many ways, and the stress and panic of the pandemic touched the mental wellbeing of people across the globe. Perhaps it is time to step back, reflect on our lifestyle and exercise, and emerge stronger on every level. Life is about training our muscles slowly. Disconnect from activities, thoughts, places and people that disturb your peace of mind — and walk towards what makes you stronger!

There are many ways of detoxing; a naturopathic diet is one. In this article we look at how homeopathy approaches detoxification, balance and wellbeing.

Homeopathy and detoxification

Homeopathy is a holistic system: it considers all the symptoms a person is experiencing and looks at the person as a whole. It is based on the principle of “like cures like”, in which a substance given in tiny doses is used for the same symptoms it is known to cause in large doses. The homeopathic view is that ill health can follow stress, long-term illness, emotional upset, poor diet and exposure to chemicals in food and elsewhere.

In homeopathic thinking, remedies are chosen to support the body’s own natural processes of elimination and to help it return to a state of balance.

There are three ways in which remedies may be prescribed in a detox programme: constitutional, psychosomatic and organ-specific.

Constitutional prescribing

This considers the whole person — physical, mental and emotional. The expression ‘treat the person, not the disease’ applies here. A single, tailor-made remedy is selected after extensive case-taking.

Psychosomatic prescribing

Here a person is seen as being out of balance first on a spiritual or emotional level, with that imbalance eventually showing itself physically. The prescription is aimed at the innermost mental and emotional state rather than the outward physical symptoms.

Organ prescribing

Organ remedies are chosen with a particular organ in mind. These are some of the remedies traditionally associated with organs in homeopathic detox programmes:

Berberis — traditionally associated with the kidneys and liver.

Chelidonium — associated with the liver and gall bladder, particularly when someone feels worse after rich food or alcohol.

Hydrastis — associated with the liver, gall bladder and intestines, and with excess mucus.

Phytolacca — associated with the lymphatic system and the glands.

Ruta graveolens — associated with the colon.

Berberis aquifolium or Calendula — associated with the skin.

Phosphorus — associated with the pancreas.

Spongia or Lobelia inflata — associated with the lungs, and often considered for smokers and ex-smokers.

Thlaspi bursa — associated with the uterus and bladder.

Nux vomica — the classic remedy picture for over-indulgence in stimulants, rich food, coffee, cigarettes and alcohol — a traditional hangover remedy!

I have mentioned only a few remedies. A detailed consultation with a qualified homeopath is the way to find the remedy that suits you (registered homeopaths can be found through the Society of Homeopaths).

In summary

The overriding definition of detoxification is removing toxins from the body. To me it also means releasing what is stuck within us on every level: physical, mental, emotional and spiritual. Homeopathy, prescribed for the whole person, aims to help us unleash, unravel and unwind anything unwanted that is trapped within us.
