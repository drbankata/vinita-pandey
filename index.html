My journey

From the laboratory to the consulting room

For seven years I was a research scientist at University College London, studying the mechanisms behind AIDS dementia in the Department of Physiology. Evidence and careful observation were my world.

Then, in 2003, my two-month-old son Pranav wouldn’t stop crying with colic — and a few small pills from my sister-in-law changed everything. I was sceptical. I was also curious. That curiosity became a career.

Questions

Good to know

What happens at a first consultation?

We talk — for up to ninety minutes. I’ll ask about your current health, your medical history, your lifestyle and the particular way your symptoms affect you. Then I choose the remedy that best matches the whole picture and explain how to take it.

Can I have homeopathy alongside my medication?

Yes. Homeopathy is complementary and does not interfere with conventional medicine. Please don’t stop or change any prescribed medicine without talking to your doctor.

Is it suitable for babies and children?

Yes — I see children of all ages, with a parent or carer present. Remedies come as small, sweet-tasting pills, granules or liquids that are easy to give.

Do online consultations work as well?

Many clients prefer them. All we need is a quiet room and a video call; remedies are posted to you or can be bought from a homeopathic pharmacy.

How many appointments will I need?

It depends. Everyday, short-term complaints may need just one. Long-standing concerns usually need a few follow-ups over several months — which is why I offer a four-month care plan.
